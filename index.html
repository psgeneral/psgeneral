Process

From first call to closeout.

Submit project details

Tell us the building type, scope, and timeline through the form below or a call — we'll confirm we're a fit before scheduling anything.

Site assessment

We walk the site, assess the facade or roof condition, and note anything that affects scope or access.

Bid & documentation

You get an itemized bid along with COI, licensing, and bonding documents for your records.

Execution

Work proceeds on the agreed schedule with a single project lead as your point of contact throughout.

Closeout

Final walkthrough, warranty documentation, and — where applicable — DOB sign-off.
