What we do

Two trades, one crew, no handoffs.

Most facade and roof failures happen at the connection between the two. Running both under one contract means fewer scheduling gaps and one point of accountability on your job.

Masonry

Masonry & Facade

  • Tuckpointing & repointing
  • Brick and brownstone restoration
  • Local Law 11 / FISP facade repair
  • Waterproofing & parapet repair
  • Lintel and shelf angle replacement
  • Sidewalk shed & scaffold coordination
Roofing

Commercial Roofing

  • TPO & EPDM membrane systems
  • Built-up & modified bitumen roofing
  • Roof replacement & recovery
  • Leak diagnosis & emergency repair
  • Drainage & scupper correction
  • Preventive maintenance contracts
Process

From first call to closeout.

01

Submit project details

Tell us the building type, scope, and timeline through the form below or a call — we'll confirm we're a fit before scheduling anything.

02

Site assessment

We walk the site, assess the facade or roof condition, and note anything that affects scope or access.

03

Bid & documentation

You get an itemized bid along with COI, licensing, and bonding documents for your records.

04

Execution

Work proceeds on the agreed schedule with a single project lead as your point of contact throughout.

05

Closeout

Final walkthrough, warranty documentation, and — where applicable — DOB sign-off.
