Roofing in Brooklyn

Built for shared walls and shared roof lines.

Brooklyn's rowhouses and brownstones often share a roof line with the building next door, which means a leak on one roof can show up as a ceiling stain two doors down. We coordinate with adjoining owners when a job touches a shared parapet or flashing detail, and we're equally at home on the flat commercial roofs and the smaller, older roof decks common across Brooklyn's brownstone blocks.
