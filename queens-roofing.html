Roofing in Queens

Bigger roofs need better drainage.

Queens' garden apartment complexes and low-rise multifamily buildings tend to have larger, flatter roof decks than a typical rowhouse — and a bigger flat roof lives or dies by its drainage. We correct scupper and drain placement to stop the ponding water that shortens a roof's life years early, and we work directly with co-op boards and management companies on scheduling, bidding, and documentation.
