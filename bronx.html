Working in the Bronx

Courtyard buildings and concrete infrastructure.

A large share of the Bronx's multifamily housing is built around courtyards and exterior concrete staircases — the kind of structural concrete work that ages differently than brick facades and needs its own repair approach: routed and sealed cracks, saw-cut nosing replacement, and expansion joint work done to spec. We've carried that same scope of work onto public infrastructure projects in the borough as well.
