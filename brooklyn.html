Working in Brooklyn

Built for the brownstone blocks.

Brooklyn's brick and brownstone stock — from Park Slope stoops to Bed-Stuy rowhouses — needs repair work matched to the original material, not a generic patch. We repoint with mortar mixed to match the existing joint, rebuild stoops tread by tread, and coordinate directly with landmarks-district requirements where they apply.
