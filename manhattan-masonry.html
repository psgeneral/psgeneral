Masonry in Manhattan

Built around the FISP cycle, not just the repair.

Most of our Manhattan masonry work starts with a Local Law 11 / FISP report flagging deteriorated brick, spalled concrete, or a failed sealant joint on a prewar elevator building. We repair to the standard the engineer's report calls for, coordinate sidewalk shed installation with your managing agent, and hand back documentation your filing can reference directly — no back-and-forth needed to close out the cycle.
