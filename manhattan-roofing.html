Roofing in Manhattan

Built for crowded rooflines and occupied buildings.

Manhattan's elevator buildings and rowhouses put different demands on a roof system than a standalone building does — crowded rooflines packed with satellite dishes, HVAC units, and shared party walls mean access and coordination matter as much as the membrane itself. We work around building management schedules and DOB permit requirements common on Manhattan roof replacement jobs, and we diagnose leaks in occupied units without guesswork or repeat visits.
